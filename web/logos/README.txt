Logos of the apps Veuly connects to. The page shows a file from this folder when it exists
(<name>.svg or <name>.png) and a plain icon when it does not. Nothing here is required.

  confluence.svg   next to Confluence, under "Connected apps"
  obsidian.svg     next to Obsidian, under "Connected apps"
  whisper.svg      not included: next to "Transcription", if you add one

No logo of an AI company is here on purpose: Anthropic's docs allow naming Claude in plain text
only, without written permission, so "Who writes your notes" and "Claude project" show a plain
icon, and other AI services are named in plain text too.

confluence.svg and obsidian.svg are copied unchanged from the Simple Icons project
(simple-icons 16.34.0, https://simpleicons.org). Simple Icons releases its files under CC0, but
the marks themselves belong to Atlassian and Obsidian: they are used here only to say which app
a setting talks about. Their owners' brand guidelines still need reading before a sale (Obsidian's
are at https://obsidian.md/brand): replace or remove a file if they ask for something else. Do
not redraw or recolour them.
